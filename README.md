# Sales-Data-Analysis-dashboard
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Sample Superstore sales, profit, discounts, and regional performance. Identifies top-performing categories, profitable regions, and loss-generating areas through KPIs, charts, slicers, and tooltips.

**📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard**

**📌 Project Overview_**

The Sample Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, discounts, and regional business trends.

The dashboard helps identify top-performing categories, profitable regions, and loss-generating areas to support data-driven business decisions.

**🛠️ Tools & Technologies**

- Power BI – Dashboard creation and data visualization
- Microsoft Excel – Dataset handling and preparation
- DAX – Measures and calculations

**📈 Dashboard Features**</br>

https://github.com/sanikabanduke/Sales-Data-Analysis-dashboard/blob/main/Sales%20data%20Analysis.pbix</br>

- KPI Cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Discount
- Category and Order Date slicers
- Profit analysis by Region and State
- Sales and Profit analysis by City
- Profit analysis by Subcategory
- Monthly Profit Trend
- Discount and Profit comparison by Category
- Interactive tooltips for additional state and city-level insights

**🔍 Key Business Insights**

1. Technology generates the highest sales and profit among all categories.
2. West is the most profitable region.
3. Phones is the highest-profit subcategory.
4. Texas records the highest loss among states.
5. Tables generates the highest loss among subcategories.
6. Fasteners records zero profit.

**💡 Business Recommendations**

- Focus on the Technology category to strengthen overall sales and profitability.
- Analyze successful business practices in the West region.
- Maintain the strong performance of the Phones subcategory.
- Investigate the reasons behind losses in Texas and the Tables subcategory.
- Review pricing, discounts, and costs for products with low or zero profit.

**🎯 Project Objective**

To transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and identify opportunities to improve business performance.
